Project Execution

6-Stage Turnkey Commercial Execution Roadmap

From initial architectural blueprint review through to white-glove assembly and sign-off, our commercial project management ensures zero site delays.

01

Discovery & Blueprint Audit

We review your CAD DWG or PDF architectural drawings to verify building columns, core electrical trench locations, and local egress code compliance.

02

3D Test-Fits & Verification

We provide full-color 3D axonometric renderings and deliver physical material swatch boxes to ensure client stakeholder alignment before manufacturing.

03

Robotic CNC Production

Orders enter our automated production line. Laser-cut steel and FSC wood cores are fabricated with sub-millimeter precision and 100% QA cycle testing.

04

Phased Dedicated Logistics

We schedule freight arrivals synchronized with your general contractor’s flooring and ceiling for the trades, preventing congested elevator bays.

05

Union White-Glove Installation

Certified technicians assemble all frames, route internal cable spines, test sit-stand motor electronics, and remove all protective packaging.

06

Punch-List Sign-Off & Warranty

On-site walkthrough with your facilities team, immediate rectification of any minor punch-list items, and issuance of the 12-Year Commercial Master Warranty.
