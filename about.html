Our 30-Year Trajectory

From Precision Steel Stamping to Global Workplace OEM

Key technical milestones that shaped our direct manufacturing approach and engineering tolerances.

1994

Foundation & Heavy Hydraulic Tooling

Founded in Chicago as an industrial structural stamping supplier. Engineered heavy-gauge steel chassis for North American contract office brands with ±0.2mm precision.

2004

Launch of Direct BIFMA Commercial Lines

Transitioned from tier-2 supplier to direct commercial manufacturer. Introduced the Modura Executive Line featuring internal cable chases and 300kg frame capacity.

2015

Smart Mechatronics & Motorized Benching

Engineered the proprietary MatrixSync dual-motor linear actuator system. Integrated sub-millisecond gyroscopic anti-collision sensors into standing workstation columns.

2021

450,000 Sq Ft Net-Zero Smart Campus

Consolidated automated Bystronic 5-axis fiber laser cutting, zero-VOC electrostatic powder lines, and robotic automated warehousing under 3.2 MW of rooftop solar energy.

2026 & Beyond

AeroSilence Acoustic Pods & IoT Telemetry

Certified ISO 23351-1 Class A sound isolation pods ($D_{S,A}$ 32.4 dB). Deployed BLE 5.3 automated facilities heat-mapping across major financial and tech headquarters.
