Contractor FAQs

Frequently Asked Commercial Questions

Answers to common logistical, architectural, and contract manufacturing inquiries.

What are standard factory production lead times?
Our in-stock Fast-Ship Express lines dispatch within 48 to 72 hours from regional fulfillment warehouses. Custom architectural veneers and tailored dimensional benching runs require 3 to 5 weeks from approved shop drawings.
Do Modura products meet ANSI/BIFMA and LEED requirements?
Yes. 100% of our desking, task seating, and storage products are independently certified to ANSI/BIFMA X5.5, X5.1, and X5.9 standards. All wooden cores are FSC-certified and emit zero formaldehyde, contributing directly to LEED v4.1 credits.
Can we provide Customer's Own Material (COM) fabrics?
Yes. We regularly accept approved COM textiles from Maharam, Kvadrat, Gabriel, and Designtex for our upholstered acoustic screens and ergonomic task seating backrests.
How does white-glove union installation work?
Our certified union assembly technicians coordinate loading dock times with your property manager, stage materials floor-by-floor, assemble desks and cable spines, complete electrical continuity tests, and remove 100% of packaging refuse.
What is covered under the 12-Year Commercial Master Warranty?
Our warranty covers all structural steel frames, welds, linear actuators, motor control boxes, and gas cylinders. In the rare event of a component failure, replacement parts are dispatched within 48 hours with on-site technician service.
